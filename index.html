Featured project

E-DOUSE – Online Laundry Service

E-DOUSE is an online laundry service platform designed especially for PG residents. It offers affordable laundry services at a lower cost and lets users place laundry orders and track the progress of their laundry.

  • 🏠 Made for PG residents
  • 💸 Affordable, low-cost service
  • 🧺 Easy online laundry orders
  • 📍 Order progress tracking

Why I built it: laundry is a daily need for PG residents, and a simple online service saves them time and money.
